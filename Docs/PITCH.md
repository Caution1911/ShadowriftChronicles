# Shadowrift Chronicles – Pitch Document

## One-Sentence Pitch
A third-person action-adventure where you wield reality itself as a weapon, phasing between overlapping dimensions to fight, explore, and decide the fate of a fractured multiverse.

---

## Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)

In *Shadowrift Chronicles*, you play as Zara Nightfall, a mercenary bound to a sentient weapon called the Voidblade. The world has been split into overlapping realities, and only by mastering seamless dimensional phasing can you survive. Every shift between layers is both a combat tool and a moral risk — push too far and you lose yourself. Your relationship with the companion AI Luna evolves based on your choices, leading to one of three very different endings.

---

## Key Selling Points

- **Seamless Dimensional Phasing** as the core gameplay loop (not a gimmick)
- High skill-ceiling combat with three distinct Voidblade stances
- Meaningful companion system (Luna Loyalty) that affects gameplay and story
- Strong visual identity across five unique realms
- Built with Meta Quest hand tracking support from the ground up
- Complete systems already implemented (phasing, combat, progression, endings)

---

## Target Audience

- Primary: Fans of character-action games (*Devil May Cry*, *Bayonetta*) and dimensional gameplay (*Titanfall 2* “Effect and Cause”, *Ratchet & Clank: Rift Apart*)
- Secondary: Players who enjoy narrative-driven action with meaningful choices

**Age Range:** 16–35  
**Platform Focus:** PC + Meta Quest (with potential for consoles later)

---

## Current Status (Prototype)

- Full Game Design Document completed
- Complete code architecture implemented in Unity
- Core systems playable (Phasing, Stances, Loyalty, Enemies, Boss, Progression)
- Meta Quest hand tracking integrated
- Scene generation tools ready
- Documentation and art direction defined

**Next Steps:** Art production, level design, audio implementation, and polish.

---

## Unique Differentiator

Most games treat dimensional travel as a story device or a slow transition.  
In *Shadowrift Chronicles*, phasing is **instant, player-controlled, and deeply integrated into combat, traversal, and narrative consequences**.
