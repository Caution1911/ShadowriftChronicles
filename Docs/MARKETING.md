# Shadowrift Chronicles – Marketing Overview

## Core Message
**Reality is a weapon. Use it wisely.**

---

## Positioning

*Shadowrift Chronicles* is a stylish, high-agency action-adventure that puts dimensional power directly in the player’s hands. It combines the fluid combat of character-action games with the mind-bending spatial gameplay of the best dimensional mechanics in gaming.

---

## Key Marketing Hooks

1. **“Phase between realities in the blink of an eye”**
2. **“Your companion is watching — and judging”** (Luna Loyalty)
3. **“Three stances. Infinite ways to dismantle your enemies.”**
4. **“Five realms. One collapsing multiverse.”**
5. **“Built for Meta Quest with full hand tracking”**

---

## Content Pillars (for social / trailers)

| Pillar              | Description                                      |
|---------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| Phase Fantasy       | Satisfying, instant dimension switching          |
| Combat Style        | Stance dancing + aggressive melee                |
| Visual Spectacle    | Reality Storm, neon rifts, layered environments  |
| Narrative Weight    | Luna’s loyalty and the cost of power             |
| VR Immersion        | Hand tracking makes phasing feel physical        |

---

## Suggested Trailer Structure

1. **Cold Open** (5s) – Zara phases through an enemy attack
2. **World Intro** (10s) – Quick cuts of the five realms
3. **Combat** (15s) – Stance switching + phasing in a fight
4. **Luna** (8s) – Show loyalty consequences
5. **Climax** (10s) – Reality Storm chaos
6. **Logo + Tagline** (5s)

**Total:** ~50–60 seconds
